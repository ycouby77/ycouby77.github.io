I'm the team's mechanical lead and I lead the chassis and drivetrain subsystem. The design decisions below are the team's; the wheels are my own design.

## Leading the mechanical side

The team has about 12 members, with 4 to 8 at a typical meeting, and most are first years without much CAD experience. I hand out research tasks, plus CAD tasks for the members who want them, and I coordinate between the subsystems. I also pointed members toward the electrical research the robot needs. A lot of my job is feasibility advice: which ideas will work, and what a design's limits are.

## Design decisions so far

- **Horizontal spinner mounted mid-robot.** Putting the weapon in the middle makes the robot more stable and the weapon easier to support.
- **Sloped armor on the sides and back.** The slopes are there to deflect hits from other horizontal spinners.
- **Invertible drive.** The wheels stick out equally above and below the chassis, so the robot keeps driving if it is flipped.

## Wheels

I designed the robot's wheels. They are cast polyurethane resin, which is the most popular choice at this weight and fits our goals best right now.

## What's next

Finish the design, then build, integrate and test the robot by around the end of February 2027, ahead of the competition in May 2027.
